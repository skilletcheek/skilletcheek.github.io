Always free in Dallas–Fort Worth

Places that cost nothing to visit on any day they are open, whatever is on the calendar above.

/ DALLAS

  • Dallas Museum of Art / Free general admission; special exhibitions are ticketed. · Arts District
  • Crow Museum of Asian Art / Free admission, in the Arts District and at UT Dallas. · Arts District
  • Klyde Warren Park / Deck park over Woodall Rodgers Freeway, with food trucks and regular free programming. · Arts District
  • African American Museum / Free admission at Fair Park, with free parking outside State Fair season. · Downtown Dallas
  • Pioneer Plaza / Forty-nine bronze longhorns and their trail riders, downtown by the convention center. · Downtown Dallas
  • Deep Ellum murals / Blocks of street murals, free to walk any time. · Deep Ellum
  • Katy Trail / Paved trail on an old rail line through Uptown, about 3.5 miles. · Uptown
  • White Rock Lake / A loop of about 9 miles around the lake for walking, running and biking. · Lower Greenville

/ FORT WORTH

  • Fort Worth Herd Cattle Drive / Longhorns down East Exchange Avenue daily at 11:30 AM and 4 PM, weather permitting; none on Thanksgiving, Christmas or Easter. · The Stockyards
  • Kimbell Art Museum / The permanent collection is free; special exhibitions are ticketed. · Fort Worth
  • Amon Carter Museum of American Art / Always free, special exhibitions included. · Fort Worth
  • Fort Worth Water Gardens / Philip Johnson's terraced fountains, downtown. · Fort Worth

/ SUBURBS

  • Arbor Hills Nature Preserve / Trails, a creek and an observation tower in west Plano. · Plano
